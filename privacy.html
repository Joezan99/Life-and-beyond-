Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy describes how Life & Beyond handles information on this website. It applies only to pages published by Life & Beyond.

Who publishes this site

Life & Beyond is an independent digital magazine. This policy is Life & Beyond's responsibility. No postal address or privacy email is published on this site.

What Life & Beyond asks for

Life & Beyond does not offer visitors an account, a comment box, a newsletter, a contact form or a checkout. It does not ask visitors to submit personal information. Reading a page does not require an account.

No products are sold on this website, so Life & Beyond does not collect payment details.

Cookies and analytics in pages published by Life & Beyond

The pages Life & Beyond publishes do not include advertising scripts or analytics scripts. They do not contain code that sets a cookie on behalf of Life & Beyond.

A short script on each page updates the copyright year and opens the mobile menu. That script runs in the visitor's browser. It does not send that information to Life & Beyond.

This is a description of the code Life & Beyond publishes. It is not a claim that a browser, a network provider or a hosting provider never stores technical information under its own rules.

Hosting

Life & Beyond publishes the pages. GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, stores those pages and delivers them to visitors. Life & Beyond does not operate GitHub's servers and does not control GitHub's systems.

Life & Beyond does not describe here what technical information GitHub processes. That processing belongs to GitHub, and GitHub explains it in GitHub's privacy statement.

Visitors who want to know how GitHub handles information connected with hosting should read GitHub's own policy. Life & Beyond is not responsible for GitHub's processing.

Links to other websites

A Life & Beyond page may link to another website. That website is responsible for its own privacy practices. Life & Beyond does not control them.

Affiliate links

Life & Beyond does not publish affiliate links at this time. If one is added later, the affiliate disclosure page will say so. Following a link can allow the other website to know that the visit came from Life & Beyond. That would be the other website's processing. It is not a record Life & Beyond keeps through this site.

Changes

If Life & Beyond starts to collect information itself, this page will be updated and the date above will change. Changes to GitHub's own practices are described by GitHub, not by this page.

Requests

Life & Beyond has not published an address for privacy requests. A request cannot be sent through the contact page until a contact method is added there.
